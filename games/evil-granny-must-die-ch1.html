👵 Evil Granny Must Die Ch1

About Evil Granny Must Die Ch1

Evil Granny Must Die Ch1 is a thrilling 3D horror survival browser game unblocked for school Chromebooks. trapped inside an abandoned creepy mansion, you must search dark rooms for weapons, ammunition, and hidden keys to escape before the terrifying Granny tracks you down.

Game Controls

  • WASD / Arrow Keys: Move around
  • Mouse: Look around & Aim
  • Left Click: Attack / Shoot
  • Right Click: Aim
  • F: Interact with items & doors
  • R: Reload weapon
  • Shift: Run

How to Play & Survive

Keep your ears open! Listen for footsteps and Creepy sounds to avoid running directly into Granny. Collect firearms to defend yourself when backed into a corner, and search every cabinet for keys required to unlock the final escape exit.
